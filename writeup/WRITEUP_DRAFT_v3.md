## Abstract

We start from the public factorized K=3 cumulant-propagation chain released under MIT by team 504aldo ("V29": raw 2.13e-8 at 0.254 B) and ask where its error is made and whether its cost can fall. **Error map (§11, measured with pre- and post-ReLU Monte-Carlo teachers of 6·10⁷ samples; the channel oracles on public MLP #0, the κ₃ oracle and the κ₄ memory results replicated on a second, synthetic 1024×16 network):** the chain's entire final-layer error lives in three numbers per neuron and layer — the pre-activation variance, κ₃ᵢᵢᵢ and κ₄ᵢᵢᵢᵢ as they enter the mean closure. Made exact one at a time they remove 47 %, 14 % and 30 % of the final MSE on MLP #0, all three together 94 % (the truth's noise floor). [25 Sep: a perfect κ₃ input (slice + diagonal) is worth ×0.61 on MLP #0 and ×0.71 on the synthetic net; the κ₄-diagonal and variance oracles are still single-network.] Both closures (the univariate mean closure and the bivariate covariance closure) are exact to the teacher's noise when fed true inputs; the error is *fresh input error born at every ReLU* — in the all-distinct entries of the post-ReLU κ₃ tensor (9–16 % wrong, about half of it beyond second order of the cumulant expansion) and in the multi-index pattern of κ₄ — that *accumulates* through the mean (gain 0.65–0.96 per layer) and the covariance. A fresh quartic response kernel plus the all-distinct K₃×C term recovers the κ₄ pattern (coefficient 1.0 ± 0.1; −8.8 % final MSE) but costs ~140 u in the current structure; every Monte-Carlo control variate we could build correlates ≤ 0.2 with the needed statistics at depth. **Cost side (§3–§8):** the "old-source tier" (107 of 260 units) cannot be made cheap — one theorem and five measurements. We ship a 4.1 % cost trim and a spherical-input reparameterization (graded 5.418e-9 → 5.137e-9) and show on a deterministic 100-network harness that every constant of the chain sits at its score optimum.

## 1. Setting (measured facts from the public material)

The task: for a He-initialized ReLU MLP of width n = 1024 and depth 16 with N(0, I) inputs, return E[h^(l)] for every layer; the score is the final-layer MSE against a 1e9-sample Monte Carlo reference, multiplied by max(0.1, FLOPs/B) with B = 2^41 FLOPs metered analytically by flopscope. We use the cost unit **1 u = one dense 1024³ matmul = 2^31 FLOPs (B = 1024 u)**.

V29 (Wu et al. arXiv:2605.05179 §4.3, "K3-simple" term selection, factorized; 504aldo's release) carries per layer the mean, the covariance C, and the third cumulant κ₃ as a **sum of sources**: each ReLU injects a source, stored as a propagator leg P_s (n×n; identity at birth; P ← Wᵀdiag(w1)P per layer), a birth leg A_s = P_s a_b, and thin n×16 legs. The next ReLU needs two slices, D3_i = κ_iii and D21_ic = κ_iic, obtained per source from contractions of Hadamard products: H = (A∘P∘w2)Aᵀ, T2 = (A∘A∘w2)Pᵀ, T3 = (P∘P)Mᵀ, T4 = (M∘P)Pᵀ, D3_i = 3Σ_j w2_j A_ij²P_ij + Σ_j M_ij P_ij². Sources older than four layers are confined to one shared rank-384 basis Q (A = Q F_A, P = Q F_P; rank-224 sub-basis after age 7) but must be **re-formed densely every layer** because A∘P has Khatri–Rao rank r² ≥ n. Cost anatomy (author's ledger, 260 u): young sources 116, old sources 107, thin/elementwise 25, covariance 7, closure 6. Empirical tolerance law: extra final MSE ≈ 4.2e-6·ε² with ε the relative RMS error of D21, so D21 must be right to ~2%.

## 2. The question the leaderboard poses

V29's bill without the old tier is 153 u; the leaders sit at 133–165 u with raw 1.64–1.9e-8 (V29: 2.13e-8). Whatever carries the deep (old-source) third-cumulant content in the leading methods costs ~nothing, while in V29 it costs 107 u for a 6× accuracy gain (no old tier: raw ~1.0e-6). We tested every way we and eight consulted models could think of to make that content cheap. All of them fail, each for a measured reason.

## 3. Theorem — there is no mean-field part (derived)

Let a source be born at layer b. For any later layer l its legs are P = Wᵀ^(l) D P′, A = P a_b, with P′, a_b and the birth coefficients w2 independent of W^(l). Every quantity linear in the source's κ₃ — H, T2, T3, T4, D3 — is therefore an **odd** function of W^(l), whereas C^(l), the leg Grams AᵀA, PᵀP and all per-neuron variances are **even**. The weight ensemble is sign-symmetric, hence

  E[ H_s^(l) | C^(l), Gram(A_s, P_s), all state at layers < l ] = 0  exactly.

Consequences. (i) Any closure that replaces old sources by a function of even statistics (mean-field, scale mixtures, "self-averaging", even monomials of C) discards the entire contribution; the measured failures of such closures — 9.1e-7 and 2.3e-6 in the author's tables — are structural, not tuning failures. (ii) Replacing a source by its expectation conditional on one fresh weight row has a relative-error floor √(6/(r+8)) in the retained support dimension r (Wick pairing: E[T(w,w,w′)] = σ²t·w′, Var ≥ 2σ⁶‖T‖²): 12% at r = 384, 16% at r = 224, an order of magnitude above tolerance. (iii) κ₄ terms built from two κ₃ contributions are even; by rotational invariance the only available even invariant is C⊗C, which explains V29's empirical regeneration law G_off = λ_l C_off (R² 0.66–0.97) as an isotropy consequence.

(The argument was obtained in an LLM consultation and checked by hand; the floor bound was derived independently by two further models with the same constant. Pilot E below shows the parity zero directly in a regression.)

## 6. Measurement C — the contracted query cannot be surrogated (measured)

The object that enters D21 is not A∘P but the contracted query U = (A∘P)·diag(w2)·F_Aᵀ (n×384). From the stored legs and basis at layers 10 and 14:

| surrogate (never forms A∘P) | layer 14 (10 old sources) | layer 10 (6 old) |
|---|---|---|
| isotropic trace U_ic ≈ (‖q_i‖²/r)·tr S_c | 98.2% | 98.9% |
| diagonal Hermite in Q coordinates (Q∘Q)D | 95.4% | 97.5% |
| degree-2 TensorSketch, width d = 128 / 512 / 2048 | 3180% / 1590% / 790% | 3280% / 1620% / 810% |
| *ceiling*: best rank-k of the exact U (r90 / r99 / r99.9) | 32 / 98 / 185; k = 128 → 6.6%, k = 256 → 1.2% | 48 / 132 / 227; k = 128 → 10%, k = 256 → 2.2% |

The trace and diagonal surrogates capture nothing (parity); the sketch error scales as 1/√d (approximate matrix multiplication of two noise-like factors) and would need d ≈ 10⁸. The result of the contraction is moderately compressible (rank ≈ 230 for 2%), but nothing that avoids forming the dense legs gets near it.

## 7. Measurement D — aggregate states (measured)

Eight consulted models converged on one surviving family: replace the old sources by k components sym₃(G_a ⊗ v_a) (exact transport G ← BGBᵀ at 2 u per component-layer) or by a pooled TT/Tucker core in the shared basis. The leaders' window above the young tier is 0–22 u, so only k ≤ 1–2 could explain them. The optimal fit is the truncated SVD of the (ab)|c unfolding of the source tensor, computable through the n×n Gram P·diag(w2)·[(AᵀA)∘(AᵀA)]·diag(w2)·Pᵀ.

| measurement (layer 14; layer 10 in parentheses) | result |
|---|---|
| cross-source response rank: energy of the first 1 / 2 / 4 modes of the normalized U_s (target 0.9996) | 0.16 / 0.26 / 0.46 (0.20 / 0.37 / 0.69) |
| vector–metric fit of the pooled old tensor, query error of H at k = 1 / 8 / 32 / 128 | 89% / 80% / 60% / 20% (– / 83% / 64% / 25%) |
| single youngest-old source, k = 64 / 128 | 18% / 4.2% |
| TT/Tucker rank of the pooled core, (a)|(bc) unfolding: error at rank 32 / 128 / 256 | 35% / 7.8% / 1.6% |

k ≈ 250–300 (rank ≈ 250) is needed for 2%: 500+ u per layer. The family is dead. (Three models predicted this range; three predicted k = 1–8 and were wrong.)

## 8. Measurement E — the slices are not a state (measured)

Regressing the old sources' H-contribution at layer l+1 on its exact slice transport (B∘B)·H·Bᵀ (B = Wᵀdiag(w1)) recovers the theoretical coefficient 1 (0.98 / 1.04) but only R² = 0.030 (layers 10→11) and 0.047 (13→14): about 95% of the next layer's old contribution comes from off-slice (a ≠ b) entries. The odd covariance feature W·C has R² = 0.0000 exactly, and B·H·Bᵀ, B·H add nothing. A recursion on (C, D21, D3) — proposed in three consultations — is hopeless.

## 10. What the leaders' formulation must satisfy (derived from §3–§8)

Any method reaching raw ≤ 2e-8 at ≤ 0.16 B carries a **realized, odd, full-rank** object whose slices are needed at every layer from every birth, at ≤ 2 dense matmuls per layer for all deep content. It is not a sum over sources; not a low-rank, diagonal, trace or sketched surrogate of the products or of their contractions; not an even closure; not a k ≤ 2 aggregate state; not a pooled core of rank < 250; not a recursion on the slices. What remains on paper: (a) a closure whose third-order transport errors are compensated inside the moment equations by its treatment of higher orders (testable by retuning the closure with the old tier removed — [OPEN], §11); (b) a source-agnostic formulation of κ₃ that never separates births (characteristic-functional or response-function type) for which no cost-complete update is known.

## 11. The complete error map (measured, 23–24 September)

**Tools.** A Monte-Carlo teacher of public mini MLP #0 (logan-fitzgerald) with 6·10⁷ input samples: at every layer the pre-activation mean, covariance, κ₃(i,i,j), κ₄(i,i,j,j), κ₄(i,i,i,j) and κ₄ diagonal, and — from the same samples — the same statistics of the post-ReLU activations at layers 3, 7, 11, 14, 15; a second teacher on a synthetic He network; the baked 10⁹-sample truth of MLP #0; probes that capture the chain's own internal quantities at every layer; and *oracles* that replace one internal quantity by the teacher's value and rerun the chain end to end. All comparisons use the plain Gaussian-input chain.

**11.1 The mean closure is exact.** The Edgeworth mean E[relu(zᵢ)] evaluated on the teacher's own marginal cumulants (μ, var, κ₃ᵢᵢᵢ, κ₄ᵢᵢᵢᵢ) matches the teacher's ReLU mean to 9·10⁻¹¹ at every layer. The per-layer MSE of the chain grows smoothly (0.7e-9 → 22e-9 over 16 layers, 100 networks): the error is accumulated, not made at the output.

**11.2 Channel oracles.** True value injected only into the mean closure, at every layer (final MSE of MLP #0; plain chain 2.290e-8):

| exact input | final MSE | ratio |
|---|---|---|
| variance | 1.220e-8 | 0.533 |
| κ₄ diagonal | 1.600e-8 | 0.699 |
| κ₃ diagonal | 1.965e-8 | 0.858 |
| variance + κ₃ + κ₄ | 0.130e-8 | 0.057 (= truth noise) |

The κ₄ number corrects our earlier record (and the ~9 % ceiling quoted from the public log, which referred to the chain's dense r = 1 core, not the true diagonal): an earlier oracle had a units bug (the chain's κ₄ diagonal and (2,2) slice are already in cumulant units; the oracle doubled one and divided the other by three). The κ₄ gain enters *only* through the mean closure (true κ₄ everywhere except the mean term: no gain).

**11.3 The bivariate closure is exact; the error is inherited input error.** With all its inputs teacher-forced at one layer, the chain's post-ReLU covariance falls to the teacher's noise (layer 7: 7.0e-5 → 2.6e-5 rms per entry, noise 2.5e-5) and the sensitivity-weighted next-layer variance error by 87 % (layer 7) / 91 % (layer 14). Forcing only the Gaussian inputs (μ, var, C) removes 62 % / 81 %, only κ₃ 28 % / 15 %, only κ₄ ~0. About 95 % of the next layer's variance error is a random projection wᵢᵀ·δC_post·wᵢ of the off-diagonal covariance error; that error is inherited (Φᵢ δC_pre,ij Φⱼ, R² 0.52 → 0.79 with depth) plus a fresh ~4·10⁻⁵ per entry. There is no missing covariance term (the audited higher Gaussian and C×κ₃₁ terms are ~3 % of the error and uncorrelated with it); the variance channel's 47 % is accumulated per-layer κ₃/κ₄ input error.

**11.4 Where the fresh error is born.** *κ₃:* the chain's κ₃(i,i,j) slice has slope 1.00 and R² 0.996–0.998 but a random 4 % → 7 % error with depth; 91–96 % of it comes from the **all-distinct** entries κ₃(hᵢ,hⱼ,h_k) of the post-ReLU tensor, 9–16 % wrong relative to themselves. It is not truncation (every source exact and untruncated: unchanged). First-order-in-κ₃ Edgeworth terms explain 35–45 % of it (the port carries exactly half of two K×C families — restoring them fully improves the slice at layers 4–10 but hurts the final mean, an open paradox); the twelve second-order K×K terms are negligible; ~55 % stays unexplained within the cumulant expansion. *κ₄:* the pairwise transport of the true post-ReLU slices, (W⁴)ᵀK₄ + 3·diag((W²)ᵀK₂₂W²), reproduces the level of the next diagonal (slope 1.00) but not its per-neuron pattern (corr 0.24–0.59); the pattern is regenerated at every ReLU in the (2,1,1)/(1,1,1,1) sectors. Linearly transported births of κ₄ (Mehler stars sharing the κ₃ legs) are right at layer 2 but ~10 % of the deep diagonal.

**11.5 Recovering the κ₄ pattern.** A fresh quartic response polynomial evaluated at every ReLU (input-κ₄ edge + Gaussian star + Gaussian path; pair part replaced by the chain's own slices; proposed in consultation) is the first construction that captures the pattern (non-pair corr 0.63–0.78 on both networks) but is 1.3× → 2.2× too small with depth. The missing piece is the all-distinct K₃×C "3+2" term evaluated on the chain's own κ₃ sources, t = ⅓[X(Pᵀx∘Yᵀx) + P(Xᵀx∘Yᵀx) + Y(Xᵀx∘Pᵀx)] per hub block minus its repeated-index part: coefficient 1.0 ± 0.1 on the residual, G⁴-weighted κ₄ error 0.124/0.142/0.193/0.210 → 0.095/0.116/0.157/0.197 (layers 4/8/12/16), final MSE −8.8 % (MLP #0). Its cost in the present structure (two products per source-layer at the query width) is ~140 u, so the recovery is not yet score-positive; cheaper variants are [OPEN].

**11.6 Sampling cannot supply the corrections (measured).** A 10⁴-sample forward costs 156 u and gives the variance to 1.4 %; the target is 0.1 %. A control variate needs a samplewise-coupled surrogate with correlation ≥ 0.997 for a 100× reduction. Measured on MLP #0 with 10⁴–2·10⁴ samples: the tangent (linearised) network fed the same input correlates with the centred second/third/fourth powers at 0.73 / 0.76 / 0.68 (layer 2) → 0.32 / 0.41 / 0.26 (layer 8) → 0.19 / 0.29 / 0.15 (layer 16); a second-order Taylor surrogate 0.967 (layer 2) → 0.73 (layer 8) → 0.00 (layer 16). Variance reductions of 1.0–2.2× at depth.

**11.7 The constants are at their score optimum (100 networks).** On the 100-network public mini split with its 10⁹-sample truth, the chain is bit-identical across runs, so paired ratios have standard errors of 0.01–0.15 %. Score ratios against the shipped chain: β 1.2 ×0.9993, λ-table ×0.9 ×1.0026, per-row λ ×1.0003, three tier-2 basis passes ×1.0006, tier-2 age gate 8 ×1.0006 (raw −0.9 % for +1.0 % cost), two tier-1 passes ×1.073 (raw −1.0 % for +8.3 % cost). Accuracy is bought one-for-one with cost in every knob.

**11.8 Where the cost goes, and the one-for-one law (measured).** A per-call-site ledger of the metered FLOPs (every matmul and einsum attributed to its source line) gives 249 u per network, 88 % in matrix products: young-source transports ≈ 54 u, young-source D21 contractions ≈ 50 u, joining a source to the shared basis ≈ 41–46 u, old sources in the basis ≈ 41 u, thin feedback legs ≈ 15 u, covariance and closure ≈ 8 u, Strassen additions ≈ 12 u. Rebuilding the shared basis at a join from the transported old basis alone saves 6.6 % of the cost but loses 1–6 % accuracy; truncating the span of the old basis plus 64 fresh directions of the joiner by the eigen-decomposition of the full core is 1 % more accurate than the range finder at +0.5 % cost (score −0.5 % on both teacher networks). Across every such change — age gates, basis ranks, subspace passes, birth-column screening, basis rebuilds, a late-layer κ₄ correction — accuracy is bought with cost at about one per cent per per cent: the public chain sits on its score optimum, and a method of equal accuracy at half the cost (which the leaderboard shows exists) must lie outside the sum-over-sources family.

**11.9 Shipped from this analysis.** Restoring a quarter of the Edgeworth K×C family that the port carries at half strength (feedback scale 1.25) together with the adaptive κ₄ exponent 1.2: 100-network paired score ×0.9946 ± 0.0007 at zero cost; graded 5.1284e-9 (raw 2.1046e-8), submission #332110.

**11.10 The κ₃ memory is long and coherent, and its problem is spatial (measured on dense source captures).** Keeping only the sources younger than h layers leaves 16 % of the layer-15 (2,1) slice at h = 10; a dressed Gaussian tree rebuilt from pair state alone (no memory) is 36–40 % wrong one step ahead. The age contributions are coherent (mean pairwise cosine 0.29–0.39; one age-mode carries ≈ 88 % of the deep total, with same-sign loadings) but not low-rank to 2 % (six modes leave 8.5 %), and the first mode is shift-stable only to 2.9 % between consecutive layers. Because transport is one linear map shared by every source, the total κ₃ is itself carried exactly as a single object; what a cheaper estimator needs is not a new temporal law but a compact spatial representation of that one cubic tensor. No linear representation of it can be both smaller and closed under arbitrary dense transport (Sym³ is an irreducible GL(n) representation, so such a state needs C(n+2,3) numbers), and the exact lazy alternative — storing only the birth matrices and the layer maps and reading the slices by reverse-mode differentiation — costs ≈ 4l square products at depth l, the same ≈ 245 u as the source chain: the two are dual contraction orders of one tensor network. A cheaper estimator must therefore change what is represented, not how the same tensor is factorised.

## 12. Open experiments and earlier negative results [OPEN]

- **Compensating closure (done, negative).** With the old tier removed the raw MSE is 7.43e-7 (32×) and no closure knob moves it: κ₄-regeneration scale 0.5–2.0 → 7.45–7.56e-7, frozen table 7.42e-7, a one-time young amplitude γ = 0.9 / 1.1 / 1.2 / 1.4 → 8.7e-7 / 6.8e-7 / 7.0e-7 / 9.9e-7 (a shallow −8 % optimum, still 30× the base). Keeping one more / one fewer age gives 4.9e-7 / 1.1e-6: the omitted content decays geometrically, ×0.66 per age, so every age is needed. (10 mini MLPs, baked 1e9 truth.)
- **Round-3 proposals (done, negative).** Two new approximations from the third consultation were falsified on the dumps: (a) *cubic source-index coding* — pooling the old sources with skew codes (Eζ = 0, Eζ³ = 1) is unbiased because the slice functional is cubic, but the cross-source interference variance is ~m× the signal: 250–530 % error with 1–3 channels, 83–130 % with 32; (b) *birth-operator compression* a_b ≈ diag(d) + UVᵀ — the birth operator is exactly the row-scaled off-diagonal post-ReLU covariance (A_s = P_s·diag(w1)C_off to 1e-5 at every layer), has zero diagonal and a flat spectrum (r99 = 310–809), and gives 57–73 % H error at k = 32, 16–22 % even at k = 384.
- **Adjoint contraction of the slices (own idea, done, negative for cost; positive for understanding).** The final mean's sensitivity to a mid-chain covariance perturbation, J_{l→16}, has 99.96 %-energy rank 944 → 173 from l = 15 to 1, so the slice products could be contracted against it at n²ρ instead of n³ — but a validated first-order response model (random δC injected into the chain: R² 0.9999 at l = 14, 0.9957 at l = 10, all path coefficients 1.00) shows that only 14 % of the response travels through that low-rank final-variance path; the rest is carried by the variance → E[relu] → mean shifts at the next two or three layers, whose sensitivities are near-range and full rank. The same model explains only 9–50 % of the exact effect of removing the old sources' slices, and a per-layer split shows why: at layers 12–14 the old tier acts 65–85 % through its diagonal D3 (n² per source) and 15–35 % through D21 (n³); both parts individually exceed the error budget at every layer (D21 3.7 / 3.1 / 1.8 / 1.3e-8 and D3 3.2 / 5.8 / 6.0 / 7.8e-8 at layers 10 / 12 / 13 / 14).
- **Learned per-neuron correction (done, negative).** On all 100 public mini networks (102,400 output neurons) the final-layer residual of the chain is unpredictable from cheap even per-neuron features: cross-validated ridge on 19 features recovers 0.3 %, a quadratic expansion 0.7 %, gradient boosting 0.5 %; per-network offsets explain 0.44 %; all single-feature correlations ≤ 0.04. This is the parity theorem seen from the data side, and it closes the observable-dictionary / Stein-state / quenched-RMT-feature families as well.
- **One-step closure floors and the copula oracle (done).** Teacher-forced on a 1024×16 network (2e8 samples per layer): the Gaussian one-step closure of the post-activation errs by 4.5–5.2e-4 RMS at layers 4–15 — three times the chain's final error — and a Gaussian copula with *exact* marginals is worse still (7.5e-4–1.1e-3): richer marginals without the odd dependence lose to plain Gaussianity. The distribution-side counterpart of the parity theorem.
- **The κ₄ channel — CORRECTED in §11.2.** *(Original text, kept for the record; its conclusion 'closed quantitatively' was wrong for the true diagonal, which is worth −30 %.)* A Monte-Carlo teacher measured the true fourth-cumulant slices per layer on two 1024×16 networks: the chain's per-layer scalar λ_l equals the mean of the true per-row weight κ₄(i)/var(i) to within 2 %, but implementing that per-row weight makes the final mean monotonically *worse* (raw 2.2911 → 2.2988e-8). The reason became public the next day: the chain propagates the **r = 1 matrix core** of the augmented chain, not the raw cumulant slices, and the scalar law fits that core at R² ≥ 0.95 from layer 3 (504aldo, finding F105, after an outside replication by AndreasHad04 showed the raw (2,2) slice obeys no such law — R² 0.998 at layer 1 decaying to 0.045 at layer 15). The same source gives the ceiling of the whole channel end to end: λ·C_off 2.23e-8, a dense exact core 2.04e-8, no channel 4.20e-8 — a perfect κ₄ core is worth ~9 % of raw MSE and costs three extra dense products per source-layer, which the budget cannot buy. The κ₄ accuracy line is therefore closed quantitatively.

- **Rank ladder (done, negative).** Lowering the shared-basis ranks loses at every step (drift vs the full chain: R_OLD2 160/128/96 → +1.2/+3.0/+7.0e-9 for −3/−4/−5 % cost; R_OLD 320/256 → +1.6/+6.3e-9 for −6/−12 %), which also rules out a pooled Tucker core (cost ∝ r³, break-even only at r ≤ 150).
- **ARC's reference chains at 1024×16 (measured, partial).** The published factorized K=3 chain (mlp_cumulant_propagation, Kind.SIMPLE, factor=True), run on two Phase-2 mini MLPs with ARC's own FLOP counter, costs **1523 u = 1.49 B** and reaches raw 3.7e-8 / 4.5e-8 against the baked truth — 5.9× dearer and 1.7× less accurate than V29. The leaders (133–165 u, raw 1.64–1.9e-8) are therefore ~10× cheaper than the reference K=3 chain: their formulation is neither the reference hub-tensor chain nor the per-source factorization. Augmented and K=4 variants do not fit at width 1024 (n³ / n⁴ intermediates), so the accuracy ladder was measured at 256×16 (Phase-1 public MLPs, first 16 layers, 1e9-sample truth): ARC K3-simple 1.866e-6 (factored = dense, 24 u), K3-augment 1.346e-6 factored / 1.246e-6 dense (200 / 219 u), V29 on the same networks 1.99e-6 (with its shape guard removed; its base path gives 5.75e-6). At 256 V29 *is* K3-simple; augmentation buys 1.5× in MSE for 8× the cost. ARC's counter attributes the factored chain's cost exactly half to leg transport and half to slice formation, both n²R — the same wall as §4–§7. K=4 rung (synthetic 128×16, 2e9-sample GPU truth): K2 7.79e-5, K3-simple 1.23e-5, K3-augment-dense 9.97e-6, K4-simple 7.60e-6 at 516 u already at width 128 (n⁴-class). The leaders' 1.3× accuracy margin over V29 equals one rung of this ladder; ARC's implementations of that rung cost 5–170× K3-simple. K4-augment — [OPEN].
- **Column screening of sources (done, negative).** Sources born deep in the network have their birth-column energy e_j = w2_j²‖a_j‖²‖p_j‖² concentrated (per-source error ≤ 1.2% at 70% of columns for births ≥ 10), and a source's transport/contraction cost is linear in its column count. End-to-end, with a mask fixed at birth, the drift against the full chain is 8.4e-10 / 3.0e-9 / 6.7e-9 for deep-birth fractions 0.9 / 0.8 / 0.7 (cost −1 / −2 / −5.6%): the error grows quadratically in the dropped fraction (tolerance law) while the saving is linear, so every schedule is net negative on the adjusted score; per-layer re-ranking does not help.

## 13. Negative results and things rejected on paper

Measured dead (23–24 September, §11): a depth multiplier on the κ₄ diagonal (its underestimate lives in neurons the closure ignores; ×1.16–1.26 worse); the literal pairwise κ₄ transport; a 13-feature regression for κ₄ across networks; linearly transported κ₄ star births; the audited missing covariance terms; low-rank corrections of the covariance error (its coherent mean-direction part is invisible to the decisive neurons; the rest needs rank ~256); every sampling / control-variate route (§11.6); an exact untruncated old tier (−3.9 % MSE for +19 % cost); a rank-128 D21 feedback (−3.1 % for +43 %); the fresh kernel alone in any late-layer or decisive-neuron restriction (net ≤ 0). Earlier: late re-formation; every single-layer and single-birth omission; trace / diagonal / sketch surrogates; k ≤ 128 aggregate states; pooled cores of rank ≤ 128; slice recursion with or without odd covariance terms; Strassen levels 6–7; all five closure knobs. Rejected on paper: a Johnson–Lindenstrauss sketch of the birth index (a linear sketch cannot preserve a trilinear sum — the reason TensorSketch failed); "birth-index-first" bilinear Gram matrices S_AA, S_AP, S_PP (D21 needs A_ij² along the birth index); an even closure λ₁C∘C + λ₂diag(C)C + λ₃diag(C)² for the odd remainder (parity); adjoint sensitivity (author's item 12; the forward covariance still needs the slices); "young-tier shadowing" (contradicted by the flat per-birth map); precision arbitrage (V29 is float32; float16 is billed as float32).
