## Disclosure policy (decided 23 Sep 2026)

Two of our results are *directly applicable* cost reductions on a public estimator that at least
thirteen accounts submit verbatim: `STRASSEN_MIN` 32 -> 8, `STRASSEN_FIRST` 4 -> 5 (together about
4.3% of compute) and the spherical-input reparameterisation (-1.1% raw). Publishing them while the
submission window is open would hand our standing to those accounts at no cost to them.

Everything else we have - the negative results, the parity argument, the kappa4 teacher, the method
map - can be published at any time without competitive cost, because it only tells people what does
*not* work.

The schedule we chose:

| When | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 23 Sep 2026 | Email the full content to `arc-whestbench@aicrowd.com` | A dated record with the organisers, who are not competitors; it also answers 504aldo's open question somewhere other than our notes |
| after 17 Oct 2026, 23:59 UTC | Post publicly on the forum | The submission window is closed, so nobody can use the cost trims against us; the write-up deadline (24 Oct) is still open, so it still counts for the contribution tracks |

The forum drafts carry an EMBARGO header stating this. Do not post them early.
