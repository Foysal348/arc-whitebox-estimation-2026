## 1. Problem and cost model (unchanged)

Random ReLU MLP, width n = 1024, depth L = 16, weights W^(l) i.i.d. N(0, 2/n), no biases; input x ~ N(0, I). h^(l) = ReLU(W^(l) h^(l-1)). Target: E[h^(16)] (1024 numbers), scored by MSE against a 1e9-sample Monte Carlo mean, under a hard budget B = 2^41 FLOPs per network with analytic metering (matmul (M,K)@(K,N) = M N (2K-1); elementwise 1/element; exp/log 16/element; float64 2x). Score = MSE x max(0.1, FLOPs/B). Unit: 1 unit = one dense 1024^3 matmul = 2^31 FLOPs; B = 1024 units.

Reference points: Monte Carlo ~1.2e-6 (budget-independent); Gaussian covariance propagation raw 4.05e-6 at 24 units; the public factorized K=3 cumulant chain "V29" raw 2.13e-8 at 250 units; leaders raw 1.64e-8 to 1.9e-8 at 133-165 units (method undisclosed). The leaders' bill equals V29 minus its "old-source tier" (107 units) to three digits.

## 2. What the K3 chain does (one paragraph)

Per layer it carries the mean, the full covariance C, and the third cumulant kappa3 as a SUM OF SOURCES: each ReLU layer injects non-Gaussianity ("source born at b"), stored as a propagator leg P_s (n x n, identity at birth, P <- W diag(w1) P per layer) and a birth leg A_s = P_s a_b. The next ReLU needs only two slices, D3_i = kappa3_iii and D21_ic = kappa3_iic, and each source contributes to them through contractions of ELEMENTWISE products of its legs, e.g. H = (A o P o w2) A^T. Sources older than 4 layers are kept as factors in a shared rank-384 basis (A = Q FA, P = Q FP), but must be re-formed densely every layer because A o P cannot be computed in factor space (Khatri-Rao rank r^2 >= n). Tolerance: D21 must be right to ~2% relative RMS (error law: extra MSE ~ 4.2e-6 eps^2).

## 3. Everything that is now CLOSED (measured on one 1024x16 network unless stated; do not re-propose)

Theory
- T1 Parity (derived): a source's contribution to D21/D3 at layer l is an odd function of W^(l); covariance, Grams and variances are even; the weight ensemble is sign-symmetric. So E[contribution | any even statistic] = 0 exactly. There is no mean-field / self-averaging part. (Corollary: kappa4 terms from two kappa3's are even, hence the empirical law kappa4_off ~ lambda_l C_off.)
- T2 (derived, two independent derivations): expectation-only replacement of an old source has a relative-error floor sqrt(6/(r+8)): 12% at r = 384, 16% at r = 224, far above 2%.
- T3 (derived): no exact identity computes (A o P) A^T for A = Q FA, P = Q FP below min(2 n^2 r, 4 n r^3) per source-layer. Earlier D3/D21 slices are not a closed state (a tensor supported on three distinct indices has zero slices now and nonzero slices after mixing).

Measurements
- M1 Spectra: old legs are low-rank (99% energy in 100-260 of 1024 directions, shrinking with age) but their elementwise products A o P, A o A, P o P are near full rank with a flat spectrum (99% energy needs 680-780 directions; top singular value ~1/10-1/30 of the Frobenius norm).
- M2 Sensitivity map (final-layer MSE of a variant vs the full chain; chain's own error 2.1e-8): keep old content only in the last 1/2/3 layers -> 4.9e-7 / 4.2e-7 / 3.2e-7 (late re-formation dead). Zero old contributions at one layer only: L5 3e-9 rising to 5-8e-8 for L10-L15 (every layer matters, effects additive). Drop one birth entirely: 1.3e-7 to 2.7e-7 for every slot 0-10 (flat; no dispensable source).
- M3 Contracted-query surrogates for U = sum_s (A_s o P_s) diag(w2) FA_s^T (n x 384) at layers 10 and 14: isotropic trace 98-99% error; diagonal Hermite in basis coordinates 95-97%; TensorSketch of the quadratic features d = 16 ... 2048: 8800% down to 790% (variance ~ 1/sqrt d; 2% would need d ~ 1e8). Ceiling: the exact U is compressible (rank ~100 for 99% energy, rank ~230 for 2%), but nothing that avoids forming the dense legs gets near it.
- M4 Strassen: level 6 breaks the 8 GB memory cap; level 7 gains nothing (block additions cancel savings). Cost trims inside the chain are exhausted at ~0.24 B.

Author's own dead list (measured by the chain's author): dropping old sources (1.0e-6); memoryless slice closure (9.1e-7; lost part is 18-23% of D21 energy, high-rank, unpredictable from live features); Gaussian scale-mixture (2.3e-6); CP-rank caps (need 8n columns); Tucker confinement (14-31x); rank/age ladders (cliffs); explicit kappa4 legs (>= 0.95 B); learned output corrections (features < 0.09 correlated with error); marginal Edgeworth / Gram-Charlier / exact Mehler on covariance propagation (4-5e-6); MC control variates (need 33-300x variance reduction, got 1.5x); adjoint sensitivity (no cost drop); Tucker cores / closed bases / hub merges (arithmetic); ~25 papers (annealed literature has kappa3 = 0; quenched stops at covariance); all sampling variants (~1.2e-6).

## 4. The one idea that survived round 1 (untested) - please develop it or beat it

"Aggregate response state": stop keeping source identities for deep content. Carry instead a few dense objects updated recursively, e.g. a vector-metric family T_old ~ sum_{a=1..k} sym3(G_a (x) v_a) with G_a (n x n), v_a (n,), whose transport under the linear map B = W diag(w1) is exact and cheap (G_a <- B G_a B^T ~ 2 units per component per layer; v_a <- B v_a), and whose slices are elementwise: D21_ic = sum_a [(G_a)_ii (v_a)_c + 2 (G_a)_ic (v_a)_i], D3_i = 3 sum_a (G_a)_ii (v_a)_i.
