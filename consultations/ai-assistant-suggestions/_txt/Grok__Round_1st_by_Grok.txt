Q3. Most informative next measurement
Proposals (a), (c), (d) are all credible on the evidence given; none can be dismissed a priori. The single
most informative next measurement is therefore the residual spectrum of proposal (c): for every old
source at layers 8, 10, 12, 14 compute the exact remainder R, its projection onto the three even
monomials of C, and the resulting relative error in D . If that error stays ≤ 2% the aggregate-slice route is

which additional even tensors are required, giving a concrete target for the next closure.
