Second Research of GPT 5.6 High Sol
Mechanistic estimation of deep third-cumulant content in random ReLU MLPs
Scope. Second-round analysis after the measured closure of low-rank product sketches, self-averaging,
source dropping, slice-only closures, Tucker/CP routes, Strassen, sampling hybrids, and learned
corrections. Claims are labeled Derived, Conjecture, or Measure-this.
Q1 - Aggregate response state: the surviving idea
[Derived] Representation. Write the deep third-cumulant tensor as a block-term / one-mode-separable
approximation
K_old ≈ Σ_{a=1}^k R(G_a, v_a), R(G,v) = sym(G ⊗ v),
with G_a ∈ R^{n×n} symmetric and v_a ∈ R^n. Under linear transport B = W diag(w1), the family is closed
exactly:
G_a' = B G_a B^T, v_a' = B v_a.
Its required slices are
D21_ic = Σ_a [(G_a)_ii (v_a)_c + 2 (G_a)_ic (v_a)_i],
D3_i = 3 Σ_a (G_a)_ii (v_a)_i.
One component can already generate a full-rank D21 because G_a is dense. Therefore the near-full spatial
rank of A∘P and the ≈230 spatial rank needed for 2% error in U do not imply k≈230. The relevant quantity
is separation rank between the symmetric two-index block and the third index.
Concrete fitting procedure
For a source at the age gate,
K_s = Σ_j d_j sym(a_j a_j^T ⊗ p_j), d_j = w2_j.
Use a two-stage fit.
Stage 1 - block-term SVD initializer. Matricize the unsymmetrized tensor as (12)|3:
M = Σ_j d_j vec_s(a_j a_j^T) p_j^T = X P^T.
The right Gram can be formed without materializing the n^2×n matrix:
M^T M = P K_X P^T, (K_X)_{jl} = d_j d_l (a_j^T a_l)^2.
Take the top k eigenvectors v_a of this n×n Gram. Recover each dense G_a from
G_a ∝ A diag(d ⊙ P^T v_a) A^T.
[Derived cost] A^T A costs ≈1 unit. The two dense multiplies for P K_X P^T cost ≈2 units. Constructing k
dense G_a matrices costs ≈k units. Initial fitting is therefore about (3+k) units once at the gate. This is too
expensive if repeated blindly in production, but ideal for the first falsification experiment.
Stage 2 - query-weighted refinement. Refine the factors directly against the four existing query channels:
min_{G_a,v_a} Σ_{m=1}^4 ||U_m - Q_m(Σ_a R(G_a,v_a))||_F^2 / ||U_m||_F^2.
For fixed v_a, the problem is linear in G_a; use variable projection or CG, then update v_a with a small
nonlinear optimizer. Because evaluating the slices of R(G,v) is O(k n^2), direct slice fitting is cheap. If the
n×384 intermediates U_m must be reproduced explicitly, fit on a fixed 64-128 column sketch and validate
on the full U_m.
[Cost] A slice-only refinement iteration is roughly 10-50 k n^2 FLOPs, i.e. about 0.005-0.025 k units/iteration.
A 128-column projected-U refinement remains below about 0.2 k units/iteration.
What k is economically plausible?
[Conjecture] Current-layer query error may need only k=1-4 even though each U has high spatial rank.
Future-stable representation is probably k=2-6. But transport dominates:
transport ≈ 2k units/layer.
 k=1: ≈20 units over ten deep layers.
 k=2: ≈40 units.
 k=4: ≈80 units.
 k=6: ≈120 units.
Thus k>4 is not economically interesting, and a leaderboard-compatible mechanism strongly points to k≤2,
or to an algebraically equivalent state with the same effective response rank.
First falsifier
[Measure-this] On one stored age-4 source (.npz legs available), fit k=1,2,4,8. Measure all four full query
errors at the gate. Then transport teacher and fitted tensor through the actual next one and three B
matrices, without refitting, and re-measure the four queries.
Kill the idea if k>4 is needed for <2% at the gate, or if k≤2 gives <2% initially but exceeds roughly 4% after
one transport. This is the highest-value experiment in this round and is comfortably under one hour on a
CPU for one source/layer.
Q2 - Other genuinely source-free formulations
1. Finite-horizon observability / balanced truncation
[Conjecture - credible] Treat deep transport as a linear time-varying system
K_{l+1} = B_l^{⊗3} K_l + S_l, y_l = Q_l K_l,
where y_l denotes the four query outputs. Instead of preserving K_l, preserve only coordinates in the
subspace observable along the actual remaining quenched weight sequence:
z_{l+1} = A_l^(r) z_l + b_l, y_l ≈ Σ_{a=1}^k z_{la} Y_{la}.
This is not the already-dead adjoint idea: the old sources are merged into one reduced global state rather
than contracted one source at a time. The relevant rank is a finite-horizon Hankel/observability rank, not
the instantaneous spatial rank of U.
[Cost] If Y_{la} are dense n×n output templates, online reconstruction is O(k n^2), below 0.001k units/layer.
A naive backward construction of block-dual modes is roughly 2k units/layer, with birth projection at most
another k units/layer. It is only interesting for k≤2.
[First falsifier] At layers 10 and 14, stack the four channel-normalized query outputs from all old sources as
samples and compute an SVD across sources. If the first two cross-source modes do not explain at least
99.96% of squared energy (the 2% RMS target), a two-state observability model is dead immediately. This
should run in minutes from stored query matrices.
2. Conditional odd-Wiener-chaos model
[Conjecture - lower probability, but not ruled out by parity alone] T1 proves E[y | even statistics]=0. It does
not prove that y is unpredictable from the realized odd current-layer weight W_l. For fixed incoming K,
(K')_{iic} = Σ_{abd} B_{ia} B_{ib} B_{cd} K_{abd}.
Decompose B_i B_i^T into its covariance plus a centered quadratic fluctuation. The covariance term gives a
first-Hermite contribution
(K')^(1)_{iic} = Σ_d B_{cd} t_d, t_d = Σ_{ab} (Σ_B)_{ab} K_{abd}.
If T2 is exactly the projection onto this first-Wiener-chaos term, then its 12-16% floor already kills the
zeroth-order version. The only genuinely new hypothesis is that, after aggregation over all old sources, the
residual Hermite-3 component has a very small conditional covariance rank.
Carry a partial-trace vector t_l plus m coefficients of residual quenched modes. The slice realization is O(m
n^2), effectively <0.01 units/layer for m≤8, provided the residual templates themselves admit a cheap
recurrence.
[First falsifier] Freeze one pre-layer old state. On 128 selected output rows, resample the current Gaussian
W_l 32-64 times, subtract the analytic first-Hermite component, and PCA the residual query responses. Kill
the idea unless the first m≤8 modes retain >99.96% of residual energy. My prior is that this fails.
3. Second-order chaos / Hessian-state formulation
[Derived rejection] Approximate each hidden fluctuation by
δh_i ≈ J_i ξ + ξ^T H_i ξ - tr(H_i).
Then
J' = D1 W J,
H'_i = D1_i Σ_j W_{ij} H_j + D2_i (WJ)_i (WJ)_i^T.
This is genuinely source-free: new births are automatically summed into H. But with chaos dimension m,
storing H costs n m^2 and transporting its output index costs about 2 n^2 m^2 FLOPs, i.e.
cost ≈ m^2 / n units/layer.
 m=100: ≈9.8 units/layer.
 m=150: ≈22 units/layer.
M1/M3 make m<<100 unlikely. This cannot explain the leaders unless a new deterministic chaos basis gives
m≲40.
[First falsifier] Project the stored old-source quadratic response onto the best m=32,64,96 deterministic
chaos directions. If m=64 is not already below 2% query error, stop. No full chain run is needed.
Q3 - Most likely explanation of the leaders
[Conjecture] The most likely mechanism is a very-low separation-rank aggregate response state, probably
k=1 or 2, or an algebraically equivalent finite-horizon response compression.
Why this matches the evidence:
 Deep-tier cost becomes independent of the number of old sources.
 Dense G can generate a high-spatial-rank D21, so M1/M3 do not rule out small separation rank.
 Linear transport of R(G,v) is exact.
 k=1-2 costs only about 20-40 units over roughly ten deep layers, consistent with a 133-165-unit total if
their young tier is also somewhat better optimized.
 It naturally explains why the leaders pay approximately the young-tier bill rather than a cost growing
with old-source count.
[Measure-this] The distinguishing observable is cross-source / temporal response rank, not singular rank of
U. At layers 10 and 14, stack the four normalized query outputs from every old source and compute the
SVD across sources. If the first 1-2 response modes explain >99.96% of squared energy, the leaderboard
mystery has a plausible mechanism. If more than four modes are required, the aggregate-response route is
economically weak; given T1-T3 and M1-M4, there is then no currently credible O(n^2)-per-layer
representation left.
Single highest-value next experiment
Fit k=1,2,4,8 aggregate response components to one stored age-4 source, validate the four query channels at
the gate, then propagate teacher and approximation through one and three actual future B matrices
without refitting. This experiment simultaneously tests separability, future stability, and the only
remaining cost regime compatible with the leaderboard.
