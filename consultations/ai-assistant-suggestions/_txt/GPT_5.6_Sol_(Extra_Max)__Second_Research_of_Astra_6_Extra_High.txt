Q3. One hypothesis about the leaders
Conjecture: they use a different coupled closure, whose errors in third-order transport are
compensated by its treatment of higher-order terms, rather than accurately reproducing V29’s
intermediate D21.
This is not the rejected learned output correction: the compensation would occur inside the evolving
moment equations. Nevertheless, I cannot name a cost-complete implementation, so this is an
explanation to test, not a proposed solution.
Measure-this: obtain one leader’s layerwise third-order corrections. If they differ substantially
from V29, replace only those corrections with V29’s values while retaining the leader’s remaining
closure equations. Worsening final MSE after that replacement would support coupled
error compensation. Agreement within 2% instead would favor a genuinely efficient
representation.
With executable leader code and existing ground truth, one V29 run plus baseline/intervention
leader runs costs at most approximately 580 units, around twenty-one minutes at 1 GFLOP/s.
Nothing currently presented constitutes a plausible complete solution at 133-165 units.
The strongest obstruction is the combination of pervasive old-content sensitivity, failure of existing
compressions, and the tiny allowance left for both aggregate transport and source absorption. The
new metric-side rank test can determine, for under eight units, whether the surviving strict
aggregate fit merits further work.
