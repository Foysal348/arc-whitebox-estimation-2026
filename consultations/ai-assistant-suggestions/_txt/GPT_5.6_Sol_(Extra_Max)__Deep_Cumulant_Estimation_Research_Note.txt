Q3. The single measurement I would make next
Measure the conditional variance of contracted old content, not another singular
spectrum of its products. Start with one age≥7 source at layer 10. This directly tests whether the
apparent product “noise” is negligible after contraction or is the missing quenched signal.
Freeze that checkpoint’s gates and source legs. Apply the gates, and obtain τ and ‖T‖² for the
resulting source tensor. They can be computed from the six pairwise leg Grams AᵀA, PᵀP, MᵀM, AᵀP,
AᵀM, PᵀM, plus elementwise contractions. This avoids materializing n³ entries. The trace follows by
contracting two legs of each symmetrized source term.
Resample 32 independent blocks of fresh weight rows, using 64 rows for the repeated index i and a
disjoint 64 for c. Compute each 64×64 exact source D21 block, and its conditional prediction
E [D21|h]. Average predictions conditionally on h, or use the analytic trace prediction directly; do
g
not compare a nonzero fixed-network tensor against an unconditional ensemble mean of zero.
On the actual next-layer weights, also record the prediction residual normalized by total D21 RMS.
Record D3 separately. Report three numbers: the analytic ρ from Q1(b), its resampling estimate
with block-level uncertainty, and the actual-network residual/total-D21 ratio. A ratio above 0.02
immediately rejects this expectation-only replacement for that contribution; a smaller ratio does not
establish that all old sources jointly pass.
FLOP budget. Six dense Grams cost about 6 units. Transporting three legs through 32 blocks of
128 rows costs 12 units. Four 64×64 slice contractions per block cost 0.5 units total. Allow about 7
units for the actual next-layer transport and slice evaluation, plus elementwise/thin overhead:
10
under 30 additional units, or 6.45×10 FLOPs. Reuse your saved checkpoint; if unavailable, add
the measured 260-unit V29 run. At a sustained 1 GFLOP/s, that is approximately 65 seconds extra,
or 10.4 minutes including the baseline.
Decision. If measured residual energy matches the derived variance and exceeds the total-slice
tolerance, stop pursuing expectation-only self-averaging. The outstanding problem is preserving
realized mixed cubic information. If it is unexpectedly small, first locate the conditioning or
cancellation responsible; that is the specific new mechanism missing from the dead closures.
Neither a flat product spectrum nor covariance alignment of a fourth-order core supplies it.
No proposed representation here has a complete, credible path to the leaders’ cost and accuracy.
The streamed identity and conditional fusion are implementation checks; the variance audit is the
highest-information research experiment.
