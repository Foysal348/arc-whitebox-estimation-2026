Executive answer. The new spectrum measurement kills low-rank approximations of A◦P, but it does not kill
compression of the quadratic-form queries that are actually contracted against the right leg. I would test, in this order:
(1) a degree-2 TensorSketch/Hermite feature representation of those queries; (2) an isotropic self-averaging closure for
the traceless “noise” part; (3) a diagonal-Hermite version in the shared basis. I do not see a new exact identity that
beats V29’s O(n2r) route without exploiting extra structure. Earlier D /D slices are not an exact Markov state.

Q2. Three hypotheses for the leaders, ranked
1. Aggregate response state (highest prior). [CONJECTURE] They may sum old-source history into 1–2 dense
response matrices updated recursively, rather than retain source identities. This uniquely matches “young-tier cost
plus almost nothing” and can preserve quenched information that a memoryless slice closure loses. Distinguishing
signature: cost becomes O(L) after the age cutoff, not O(L2) in source-layer pairs; deterministic error; increasing depth
from 16 to 24 should add roughly a constant 1–2 units/layer, not an expanding old tier.
2. Quadratic-feature sketch of old contractions. [CONJECTURE] This is Proposal A with d≈64–128 and
aggressive aggregation across sources/families. Signature: a smooth accuracy-versus-d curve and seed-to-seed variation;
local D21 error is nearly zero-mean. At d = 64, layer-14 contraction cost is only 0.82U for rank 224 before factor
transport.
