4. What I think the leaders could be doing
[Conjecture] If the source-index coding experiment succeeds with two or three channels, it is the first mechanism
in these three rounds that naturally explains both signatures: (1) cost nearly independent of the number of old
births, and (2) preservation of the actual odd, full-rank fluctuation rather than its zero conditional mean. A
practical leader implementation could keep the newest one or two sources exact and pool the rest into one or two
skew-coded channels; four total channels cost about 8.16 u/layer for all K3 work and land naturally in the observed
133–165 u range once the rest of the chain is added.
The remaining 1.3x accuracy advantage over V29 does not require a full K4 tensor. After halving K3 cost, even a
local/one-step augmented correction costing one extra Strassen product per layer is only 0 .51× 16≈ 8.2 u total.
That is the scale I would allocate to the extra rung before considering any transported n4 state.
If coding fails: then I cannot beat the practical two-product/tangent floor with the allowed operations. The
strongest argument is that the hard query is the action of a high-rank symmetric-square coefficient operator on a
realized second-chaos feature, while the repeated-leg structure only removes the second slice product when the
third leg is effectively diagonal. Your measurements rule out every known low-rank replacement of that coefficient
operator. In that case the only credible bill-halving approximation is exactly the query-optimal diagonal-third-leg
test above; if that also drifts above 2%, I would regard the leader mechanism as requiring a qualitatively different
approximation not represented in the current K3 source algebra.
