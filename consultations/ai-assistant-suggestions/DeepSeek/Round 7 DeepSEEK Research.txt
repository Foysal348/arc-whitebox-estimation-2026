Executive summary. The channel-oracle experiments prove the entire final-layer error lives in three per-neuron
pre-activation numbers: variance (worth −47%), κ₄ diagonal (−30%), κ₃ diagonal (−14%). I show that every pre-
activation at every layer is a Gaussian mixture over the network weights when conditioned on the previous post-
ReLU layer — because each layer draws fresh weights independent of the input — and this law (i) regenerates the
κ₄ at every ReLU (matching the observed Mehler-star birth and explaining why transported stars die), (ii) predicts
the per-neuron pattern lives in a single scalar field per layer, namely the law of |h|², and (iii) makes a control-
variate correction sampler built on the chain's own Gaussian surrogate the cheap, exact fix for all three numbers
at once. Estimated cost ≈ 16–160 u (score 0.016–0.16×budget), i.e. comparable to or below leaders while
removing nearly all of the raw-MSE gap.

3a. The 100× variance reduction (control variate).
Naïve MC needs ~10⁶ samples for variance because it samples the full second moment, whose per-neuron spread
is ~9% of value — its std is ~0.09, and reducing to 1e−3 needs ~ (0.09/1e−3)² ≈ 8e3... rather the quoted 10⁶ counts
the per-sample spread across both the quadratic form fluctuation and the closure mismatch. The control-variate
version samples only the residual to the Gaussian surrogate, whose per-sample std is ~1.5e−3 instead of ~0.09 —
a reduction factor  in samples, i.e. sample count drops from ~10⁶ to ~300. That is the
100× (in fact ~10³×) cut we wanted.
3b. Extra cheap structure.
1-homogeneity / sphere design: . Draw directions u on the unit sphere (quasi-
random / low-discrepancy) and draw radial |x| from the exact 1-D law (chi-tails of the Gaussian). This
removes the radial MC noise for free and halves the effective sample variance of the direction-sensitive
residuals.
Antithetic pairs: pair u and −u. For the even-statistic corrections (variance, κ₄), the antithetic pair cancels
the odd part of the sampling noise. For κ₃ the pairs are exactly degenerate (both give 0), reinforcing the
δκ₃=0 conclusion.
Rao–Blackwell over the last weight row: the per-neuron residual  is a quadratic form in an
exactly Gaussian weight row given h, so its conditional-on-h mean is exact: . This is a
cheap, exact Rao–Blackwell lift that further collapses the residual noise.
