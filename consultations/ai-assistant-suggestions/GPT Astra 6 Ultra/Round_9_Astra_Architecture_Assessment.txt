Why I do not offer two more “new” architectures
Derived: explicit all-distinct birth. A packed symmetric K₃ has 179,481,600 float32 entries, about 0.718
GB. The direct three-mode transport algorithm costs ≈3n u = 3072 u per layer classically, or ≈1567 u using

Derived: reverse cubic-functional evaluation. A birth polynomial g(q)=Σⱼβⱼqⱼ(aᵀq)ⱼ² can be retained as a

Derived: young-source arithmetic. Even deleting all 116 u of young work would leave 144 u. Adding a

improvement is the wrong optimization target, and the identified partner channel should determine the
next architecture. This diagnostic is more informative than another ungrounded tensor ansatz.

Scope of verification
A small independent NumPy check used a random n=12 tensor, not a neural-network teacher. It verified
the mixed-coordinate slice identity, curvature-query identity, newborn identity cancellation,
covariance-response reuse and the all-distinct subtraction. Maximum absolute discrepancies were
1.1-2.2×10⁻¹⁶ in float64. This validates those algebraic identities only. It does not validate the complete
quartic kernel, float32 behavior, network accuracy, runtime or memory.
No Round 9 teacher arrays or complete 140 u response implementation were supplied. The terminal cost is
therefore an explicit inventory estimate, and edge/pair-matching overhead remains an item to meter. The
report does not assert that the 20 u target has been met.
What is new in this response
The proposed computational boundary is a mixed-coordinate response: two legs have crossed the next
weight matrix and the third has not. It exposes a new-direction K₃ query after the per-source work has been
summed, while a newborn identity leg cancels the additional aggregate transport in full-slice layers. This is
a different split of the work, not a new statistical approximation to old sources.
What remains unsolved
The baseline source-transport cost, the unexplained cubic all-distinct fresh error, and a validated joint
improvement of all three marginal inputs remain unsolved. The compensation experiment identifies where
a joint redesign would have to act. Neither the exact identities nor the cited literature establish a 150 u
estimator.
