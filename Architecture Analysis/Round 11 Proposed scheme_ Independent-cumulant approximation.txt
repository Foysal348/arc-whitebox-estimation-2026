Cost: In n³-unit terms, propagating Σ′ costs one n³ (matrix multiply) and propagating d₃ costs another n³.
Mean updates are ≪n³. Total ≈2n³ products (≪125u), satisfying the budget. 
Falsifier: A simple decisive check is to compare the scheme’s predicted κ₃-channel against the teacher’s
data. For example, at layer 11 compute Y₂₁(i,j)=κ₃(i,i,j). Our scheme assumes off-diagonal κ₃=0, so it predicts
Y₂₁ off-diagonals = 0. The teacher’s Y₂₁ at that layer is nonzero, so the relative Frobenius error on off-
diagonals would greatly exceed the chain’s ~5% tolerance. Thus a one-step test of Y₂₁ at L11 (or similarly
D_{21} at L15) would falsify this scheme by a wide margin. (In other words, neglecting cross-neuron third
cumulants causes unacceptably large errors.) 
Missing term: To approach the chain’s accuracy one would need to restore the omitted cross-cumulant
terms (the off-diagonal K31 entries). Including a low-rank or structured approximation of those κ₃(i,i,j)
contributions would close the gap.
