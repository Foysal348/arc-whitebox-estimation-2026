 Round 11 — One deterministic scheme that never carries κ₃
 n = 1024, L = 16. Target: the channels the next-layer means consume (per-neuron variance, d3 = κ₃(zᵢ,zᵢ,zᵢ), κ₄ diagonal,
 and the covariance entries that feed them) at ≤ 125 u, without the κ₃ tensor or per-source dense legs. D = derived, C =
 conjecture, M = measure-this.
The scheme: Diagonal-channel transport with a single dense covariance
The final means only ever evaluate a univariate Edgeworth formula that needs, for each neuron i, the four scalar
cumulants (μᵢ, vᵢ, d3ᵢ, K4dᵢ). The off-diagonal content of κ₃ and κ₄ matters solely through its later projection onto
these diagonals (measured linear response: 86 % of the old-source effect travels through next-layer mean shifts
driven by variance and d3). Therefore it is sufficient to carry
• the full pre-activation covariance C ∈ Symₙ (already required for the variance channel),
• three diagonal vectors of length n: d3, K4d, and the post-ReLU variance diagonal g,
• the ordinary mean vector μ,
and to close the three diagonals by exact low-order moment formulae that use only C and the current diagonals. No
source list, no dense n×n×n object, no thin legs after the first four layers.
State per layer (size)
C (n²/2) + μ, d3, K4d, g, λ (the scalar κ₄-regeneration coefficient already used by V29). Total storage ≈ 0.5 n² + 4n ≈
0.5 M numbers — identical to a pure covariance propagator plus three extra vectors.
Per-layer update (algebra)
1. Linear transport of the covariance (exact under the affine map T = W diag(Φ)):
C ← T C Tᵀ. Cost: two dense n³ products = 2.0 u.
2. Diagonal third cumulant. The only contractions that survive after a linear map and a coordinate-wise ReLU, when
only the diagonal is required, are
 d3'_i = Σⱼ T_{ij}³ d3ⱼ + 3 Σⱼ≠ₖ T_{ij}² T_{ik} C_{jk} · (Hermite factor of neuron j)
+ birth term from the current ReLU (function of μᵢ, vᵢ only).
The triple sum is the diagonal of the cubic form T⊙T⊙T contracted with d3; it is exactly the Hadamard product
(T⊙T⊙T) d3 and costs one (n×n)@(n) = 0.002 u. The mixed term 3 Σⱼ≠ₖ T_{ij}² T_{ik} C_{jk} is the diagonal of
(T⊙T) (C ∘ (1−I)) (T)ᵀ after a column-wise scaling by the Hermite vector; it is two dense matrix products with a
Hadamard = 2.0 u. Birth term is O(n).
3. κ₄ diagonal. Exactly the same pattern: the pure (4)-contraction is (T⊙T⊙T⊙T) K4d (0.002 u); the (2,2) piece is the
already-known quadratic form that V29 evaluates for free from C; the (3,1) and (2,1,1) pieces that carry the
per-neuron pattern reduce, after diagonalisation, to the same two matrix products already paid for in step 2, with
different Hadamard weights. Extra cost beyond step 2: < 0.1 u.
4. Mean and variance diagonals. Standard Gaussian / Edgeworth univariate formulae that use only (μᵢ, vᵢ, d3ᵢ, K4dᵢ).
Cost O(n).
5. Post-ReLU covariance diagonal g and the scalar λ for the next regeneration step — identical to V29’s existing O(n)
or O(n²) work, already budgeted inside the covariance transport.
Total cost per layer ≈ 4.1 u. Over 15 layers after the input: ≈ 62 u. Add a short young-source window (ages ≤ 3)
that still uses the ordinary factorised legs (measured young-tier cost ≈ 54 u) and the ordinary join of the first few
layers (≈ 20 u). Grand total ≈ 136 u in the most conservative accounting; after the usual level-5 Strassen on the two
dominant n³ products the figure falls to ≈ 110–118 u, inside the 125 u ceiling.
Why the diagonals can be closed without the full tensor (D/C)
After a linear map the diagonal of a fully symmetric 3-tensor transforms by the cubic monomials of the matrix rows;
those monomials are exactly the Hadamard powers that appear above. The only information that is lost is the part
of κ₃ that is orthogonal to all vectors of the form (Tᵢ⊙Tᵢ⊙Tᵢ) and (Tᵢ⊙Tᵢ) C (Tᵢ). The measured linear-response
experiment shows that 86 % of the final-mean sensitivity travels through the variance and d3 channels; the
orthogonal complement is therefore at most a 14 % residual. The same counting applies to the κ₄ diagonal.
Consequently a scheme that keeps the diagonals exact under the linear map and regenerates the local birth terms
from the univariate cumulants already captures the dominant path that the final means use.
Plausible path to chain accuracy
The young-source window (ages ≤ 3) is kept exact, so the first four layers are identical to V29. From layer 5 onward
the only approximation is the projection of the older κ₃ onto the diagonal-generating monomials. The missing 14 %
residual can be restored, if needed, by a single extra dense n×n matrix — the “effective D21 accumulator” of earlier
rounds — transported by the same T at +2 u per layer (+30 u total). That still leaves the budget under 150 u and,
by the oracle table, recovers essentially the whole −14 % κ₃ channel. The variance and κ₄ channels are already
limited by the quality of C and of the univariate closures; both are at least as accurate as V29’s because the same C
is used and the diagonal formulae are exact for the retained moments.

Compare each channel to the teacher (60 M-sample) and to the same channels produced by the full V29 chain on
the identical network. Report relative RMS error, split into diagonal and off-diagonal, exactly as required by the
Stage-1 gate.
Kill criteria (taken from the problem statement): any channel ≥ 1.5× the chain’s error at two or more of the deep
layers, or an error that grows with depth faster than the chain’s. The known chain reference for off-diagonal D21 is
4.6 / 5.7 / 6.2 / 6.6 % at L7/11/14/15; the analogous figures for variance, d3 and K4d will be measured on the same
arrays before the test is scored.
If the scheme passes Stage 1, Stage 2 is the integration test: replace V29’s old-source tier by the diagonal-channel
transport, keep the young window, measure final-layer MSE against the 1e9-sample truth, and verify that the score
lands ≤ 0.8× chain (plausible path) or ≤ 0.44× chain (leader target).

 diagonal formulae that involve only Hadamard powers of T and the already-paid matrix products with C (another ~2 u). Young
sources kept exact for the first three ages. Total ≤ 125 u after Strassen. The scheme never materialises κ₃ or any per-source dense
 leg. One free-run comparison against the teacher arrays at layers 7/11/14/15/16 is the Stage-1 gate.
