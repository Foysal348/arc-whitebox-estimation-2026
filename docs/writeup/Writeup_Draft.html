0. Summary

We start from the public factorised third-order cumulant chain of team 504aldo (MIT), which propagates the mean, the covariance and the third cumulant (as a sum over "sources" born at each ReLU) through a 1024-wide, 16-layer bias-free ReLU network. We add one exact identity (a spherical-input reparameterisation, section 2) and some cost engineering. Most of this report is a measured map of where the remaining error comes from, and why it resists every cheap fix we and others tried:

  1. The closures are essentially exact given true inputs. The error is made by the inputs: a perfect third-cumulant input (slice + diagonal) removes 39% of the final MSE (x0.61 on one network, x0.71 on a second), a perfect fourth-cumulant diagonal 30%, and forcing the true mean at every layer 81%. The gain is spread over layers 8-16.
  2. That input error is carried memory, not fresh physics. Erasing all non-Gaussian history h layers back recovers only 2-6% of the deep fourth cumulant at h = 2 and 20-35% at h = 6; the third cumulant behaves the same way (keeping the sources of the last 10 layers still leaves 16% of the (2,1) slice at layer 15).
  3. The carried content is odd in the later weights, so any statistic that is even in them (per-neuron variances, kurtosis, ensemble averages) has zero conditional correlation with it (parity theorem, section 5).
  4. It is not compressible in the ways that would make it cheap: the elementwise products the chain needs are full-rank with a flat spectrum; no linear state closed under the dense layer maps is smaller than the symmetric cube; every contraction order of the young-source tensor network costs the same 193 square products.

1. Setting and baseline

  • Task: estimate the final-layer post-ReLU means of a He-initialised bias-free ReLU MLP (n = 1024, L = 16, x ~ N(0, I)); score = MSE x max(0.1, FLOPs / 241).
  • Baseline: 504aldo's V29 (github.com/504aldo/whest-p2-cumulant-k3, MIT): a port of the factorised K = 3 algorithm of Wu et al. (arXiv:2605.05179) with a memoryless, regenerated fourth cumulant, a bivariate Wick/Edgeworth closure, dense legs for sources of age ≤ 4 and a shared rank-384 (nested rank-224) basis for older sources. Public score 5.40e-9.
  • Our changes (graded): Strassen block-size and first-call trims (util 0.254 → 0.244; 5.418e-9 → 5.195e-9); the spherical input reparameterisation (section 2; raw −1.1%, 5.137e-9); the feedback constant 1.25 and adaptive-lambda exponent 1.2 chosen on 100 local networks (5.128e-9).

2. An exact identity: the spherical input

Write the Gaussian input as X = (R/√n) Y with Y uniform on the sphere of radius √n and R ~ χn independent of Y. A bias-free ReLU network h is positively homogeneous of degree 1, so E[h(X)] = a1 E[h(Y)] with a1 = E[R]/√n = 0.99975589 at n = 1024. Y has covariance I, zero odd cumulants and a small negative, exactly known fourth cumulant (c = −2/(n+2) per Sym(I⊗I) pair), which we inject at layer 0. Propagating Y instead of X removes the radial fluctuation from every layer's input statistics.

  • Result: raw final MSE −1.1% on the grader (5.195e-9 → 5.137e-9), zero extra FLOPs.
  • Credit: the idea of an angular input appears in barnobarno666's public repository; the derivation of the layer-0 fourth cumulant and its injection into the K3 chain are ours.
Hand-check: the constant c and the layer-0 core g = −6/(n+2) in the estimator.

An exact contraction-order search (forward, reverse mode, mixed, interval maps, on-demand legs, batching) finds the same 193 in every order: the elementwise (Hadamard) hyperedge must be materialised before each n3 contraction.
  • Every knob inside the chain (age gates, basis ranks, range-finder passes, feedback rank) trades about 1% cost for 1% accuracy on a 100-network paired harness: the chain sits at its own score optimum.

4. Where the error is made

4.1 The closures are exact; the inputs are not

  • Teacher-forced closure (all inputs from a 6x107-sample Monte-Carlo teacher at one layer): the post-ReLU covariance error falls to the teacher's noise.
  • Channel oracles (teacher value injected at every layer, final MSE ratio, MLP #0 only so far): variance x0.53, fourth-cumulant diagonal x0.70, third-cumulant diagonal x0.87, all three x0.057.
  • A perfect third-cumulant input, the (2,1) slice κ3(zi, zi, zj) plus its diagonal: x0.608 (MLP #0) / x0.715 (a second, synthetic network). The slice alone: x0.758; the slice fed only to the chain's feedback legs: x0.9999.
  • Forcing the true mean at every layer: 2.24e-8 → 4.2e-9. About 80% of the final error is mean error inherited from earlier layers.

5. The error is carried memory, and it is odd

  • Method: at layer L−h, replace the pre-activation by a Gaussian with the true mean and covariance, run the true network h steps, compare the κ4 diagonal with the teacher; "all" carries the true κ4 of layer L−1 linearly. Fresh ReLU physics from a Gaussian input gives 1-4% of the deep level.
  • Third cumulant: keeping only sources of the last 2 layers leaves 84% of the (2,1) slice at layer 15, the last 10 layers still 16%. One age mode carries ~88% of the old content (it is coherent), but six modes still leave 8.5% (it is not low-rank in age); the total κ3 is transported exactly by one linear map, so the difficulty is spatial, not temporal.
  • The next layer's κ3 diagonal is fed 90-99% by off-diagonal κ3, and the next κ4 diagonal ~90% by the (2,2) pair term; even the exact repeated-index slices miss 22-29% (κ3 diagonal) and 36-40% (slice) one step ahead: the missing part is the all-distinct content.

5.2 Parity

The contributions of old sources to the next slice are odd functions of the later layer weights. Their conditional expectation given any statistic that is even in those weights is zero. Consequences, each confirmed by measurement: per-neuron features correlate ≤ 0.04 with the residual (102,400 neurons); marginal (copula) corrections with Gaussian dependence are worse than none; ensemble / mean-field closures cannot see the content; the fitted relation κ4,off ≈ λl Coff captures level but not pattern.

Write out the proof (two independent derivations exist in our consultation logs) and the floor √(6/(r+8)) for expectation-only replacements; hand-check.

7. Open problem

A method one rung more accurate than this chain must represent the all-distinct third- and fourth-order content born at every ReLU and carried across ≥ 6 layers, without the elementwise products of per-source legs. Our measurements say what such a state must reproduce (section 4), how long it must remember (section 5.1), and that no statistic even in the weights can stand in for it (section 5.2).
